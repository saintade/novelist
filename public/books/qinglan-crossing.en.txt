CHAPTER 1. The Ferry Ledger

At dawn, Lin Yao arrived at Qinglan Crossing with an old ledger in his hands. The ferry had not yet begun its run. Dew on the stone steps caught the faint light.

Gu Ning sat beneath the eaves, repairing the Moonlit Lantern. A disciple of Rainwatch Pavilion, she kept the daily record of boats passing through. Lin Yao set the ledger on the table. He had learned only the first lesson of the Breath Condensation Realm, he said, and could not yet keep the wick bright.

Gu Ning did not light it for him. She asked him to count the three ripples on the river and breathe slowly. After his second attempt, the Moonlit Lantern shone for an instant.

CHAPTER 2. A Name in the Rain

A light rain began in the afternoon. The people at the crossing called Lin Yao A-Yao, though he always wrote his full name carefully in the ledger.

Gu Ning taught him the Returning Wind Art. It was not a way to command the wind over the river, she said, but an exercise for settling the breath. New disciples of Rainwatch Pavilion learned it before the other lessons of the Breath Condensation Realm.

Only after three attempts did Lin Yao realize that the Moonlit Lantern did not have to shine all the time. He placed it by the window at Qinglan Crossing and lit it only when someone asked about the ferry schedule.

CHAPTER 3. The Second Ledger

On the third day, Gu Ning brought a new ledger. She left the old one with Lin Yao and asked him to check the season's ferry schedule at Qinglan Crossing.

Lin Yao wrote his name on the first page, then added A-Yao beside it. This time, the familiar name no longer felt separate from his own.

A letter from Rainwatch Pavilion said that the assessment for the Breath Condensation Realm would not take place until next month. Gu Ning put the letter away and practiced the Returning Wind Art as usual. The Moonlit Lantern sat quietly on the table. Neither of them hurried to light it.
